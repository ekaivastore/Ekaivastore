Questions, answered

Quick answers about buying and using Ekaiva.

Where can I buy Ekaiva?

Ekaiva is sold on Swiggy Instamart. Open the Swiggy app, tap Instamart and search for “Ekaiva” or the name of the masala. Availability depends on your delivery area.

Can I order directly from this website?

The website does not take payments. You can buy on Swiggy Instamart, and each product page has a button that takes you there. If you see + Add to order buttons, you can also add products, enter your address and send the order to us on WhatsApp — it is confirmed once we reply.

Is Ekaiva FSSAI registered?

Yes. Our FSSAI registration number is 21522020001385.

What pack sizes are available?

All our products are currently in 100 g packs.

Do you supply to shops, restaurants or distributors?

Yes, we welcome those enquiries. Please use the contact page and tell us what you need and where you are based.

How should I store my spices?

Keep the pack closed tightly in a cool, dry place away from moisture, heat and direct sunlight, and use a dry spoon.

I have a problem with an order I placed on Instamart.

Please contact Swiggy Instamart support from the app for order, delivery or payment issues. For feedback about our products, write to us on the contact page.
