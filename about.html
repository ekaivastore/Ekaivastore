Our story

A spice brand for everyday kitchens

Ekaiva is a spice brand from Bhayander West, Mira Bhayander, in Maharashtra. We make masala blends and pure spice powders for everyday Indian cooking — nine products today, from Kitchen King and Garam Masala to Chat Masala, Kashmiri Chilli Powder and Turmeric.

Our line, swaad hai toh baat hai, is the standard we hold every pack to: if the taste is right, everything else follows.

You can find Ekaiva on Swiggy Instamart, and we welcome enquiries from retailers, restaurants and distributors.
